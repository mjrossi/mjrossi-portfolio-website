This is the first entry of a small, slow-moving writing space attached to the
rest of the site. The plan is simple: occasional notes on software engineering,
urban mobility, and the in-between places where the two meet.

## What to expect

- Short pieces on transit data, GTFS feeds, and the tools around them.
- Notes on software craftsmanship — the kind that doesn't fit in a commit message.
- The occasional reflection on city life, cycling, and getting around without a car.

## How it's built

Posts are MDX files in the repository. Publishing is a commit and a push. The
content pipeline is [Astro Content Collections](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/),
the site is static, and everything is served from the edge.
